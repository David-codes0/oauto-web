The right craftsman, and the right part, without asking around.

OAuto is a vehicle-help and parts marketplace for Nigeria. Motor owners find a nearby mechanic, vulcanizer, panel beater, auto electrician, or painter. Craftsmen show their work and list spare parts. Part sellers see those posts and message the person who needs the part.

What we do

Three people, one place to get a car moving again.

Motor owner

Find help for this vehicle.

Your home screen shows where you are. You choose the craftsman the car needs, then search the nearest one on a map. Tap a marker and pay to reveal the name, phone, and workshop. If the job is a part, upload photos and details so sellers can reach you.

Craftsman

Show the work, then list the part.

Mechanics, vulcanizers, panel beaters, rewire electricians, and painters open on their own record: name, trade, rating, and completed repairs. From that page they photograph a spare part and add the details buyers need.

Part seller

See new parts as they are posted.

The seller home is a feed of uploads from motor owners and craftsmen. Each post shows the part, the vehicle, and the photos. Message the uploader, agree a price, and complete the sale.

The people on the ground

Mechanics, vulcanizers, and the rest of the yard.

These are the trades OAuto is built around. The Android and mobile apps will put each of them a search away.

Coming soon

Android and mobile, on the way.

The OAuto app is in progress for Android and for mobile. Motor owners, craftsmen, and part sellers will use the same flow on the phone: search, reveal, post a part, and message. Creating an account on the web requires a verification code sent by SMS before the account can open.
